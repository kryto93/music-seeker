App

Run MusicSeeker as a standalone app with its own window. The app connects to the server — no updates needed when the backend changes.

Installing from inside the app replaces the bundle and clears macOS quarantine for you. A copy downloaded through a browser instead opens as "MusicSeeker is damaged" — clear it once with sudo xattr -cr /Applications/MusicSeeker.app.
